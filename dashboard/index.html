Method

How this was fetched.

A short, inspectable method: public pages only, one capture time, explicit gaps, and no synthetic records. If a number is not in the source, it is not in the dashboard.

Capture: — 177 records No estimates

What was fetched

  • Instagram public profile metadata for 4 accounts.
  • X public profiles for 5 accounts, plus 7 dated posts with public metrics and 5 undated profile posts.
  • Telegram public channel preview: 125 messages.
  • YouTube channel about pages and the latest 15 videos for 3 channels.
  • Facebook page titles for 2 pages; no counts or posts.

How it was fetched

  • Logged-out HTTP requests only. No account, cookie or token was used.
  • Instagram profile metadata came from the public link-preview surface.
  • X profiles and posts were read through a rendered public page.
  • Telegram came from the official t.me/s/ preview.
  • YouTube came from the public about page and RSS feed.

What is counted

  • Followers / subscribers: as displayed at fetch time.
  • Posts: account post count, or fetched items where marked.
  • Views: as displayed on the post/message/video.
  • Items: one row per fetched message, video or post.
  • n/e: not exposed by the platform.

What is not

  • No reach, impressions or unique audience.
  • No sentiment, stance or bot classification.
  • No estimate for blocked Instagram/Facebook/Reddit data.
  • No claim that followers equal supporters or votes.
  • No private groups, DMs or WhatsApp content.
The integrity rule

Every record carries a platform, account, timestamp (where exposed), source URL and capture time. If a platform did not return a field, the dashboard shows “n/e” or a blocked status. There are no placeholder posts, no synthetic engagement figures and no extrapolated totals.

Refresh recipe

Repeat the same logged-out capture and rebuild the dataset; do not mix capture dates.

Limits of this snapshot

This is one capture, not a time series. Instagram and Facebook post-level data are missing; X exposes only recent posts for two accounts; Telegram's public preview ends on 6 October 2026. The 177 items are a window into public activity, not the whole conversation.
